I am an **Assistant Professor (Maître de conférences) at Télécom Paris (Institut Polytechnique de Paris)**, working on **multimodal artificial intelligence for robust clinical decision support**. My research focuses on the integration of **medical imaging and clinical data**, with applications in **cardiology** and **breast cancer**, aiming to improve the reliability, generalisability, and fairness of AI-based clinical models.

Prior to this position, I was a **postdoctoral researcher in cardiac imaging** at **Helmholtz Munich** and the **Technical University of Munich**, in the group of **Prof. Julia Schnabel**. My work centred on **representation learning** for cardiac imaging and multimodal data.

I completed my **PhD in Lyon** under the supervision of **Nicolas Duchateau** and **Patrick Clarysse**, where I investigated **manifold learning approaches to model and analyse the cardiac function**.
