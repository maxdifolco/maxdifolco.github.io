Maxime Di Folco, PhD

Associate Professor in Computational Biomedical Imaging

LTCI, Télécom Paris, Institut Polytechnique de Paris

I am an Associate Professor (Maître de conférences) at Télécom Paris, in the IMAGES team of the LTCI laboratory, working on multimodal artificial intelligence for robust clinical decision support. My research integrates medical imaging and clinical data, with applications in cardiology and breast cancer, aiming to make AI-based clinical models more reliable, generalisable and fair.

Before this, I was a postdoctoral researcher in cardiac imaging at Helmholtz Munich and the Technical University of Munich, in the group of Prof. Julia Schnabel, working on representation learning for cardiac imaging and multimodal data. I did my PhD at Université de Lyon (CREATIS) with Nicolas Duchateau and Patrick Clarysse, on manifold learning to model and analyse cardiac function.

        Research

        My research focuses on representation learning for medical imaging, with a particular interest in multimodal learning: combining medical images with the clinical data that come with them to improve diagnosis and give a more complete description of each patient.

        My main application is cardiology, where I also work on the analysis of cardiac shape and deformation. Breast cancer is a second application. Across both, I aim to build models that are robust, including when clinical data are incomplete, and fair across patient populations.
